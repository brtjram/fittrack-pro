Notes on light mode
Cards need shadow, not surface. In dark mode a card is a lighter rectangle. On light, white-on-warm-white needs a 1px + 24px shadow pair to read as lifted.
Both accents darken. Amber goes #F59148 → #C4551C for fills, so white button text clears 4.5:1 — and a step further to #A8410F when amber is used as text on paper (5.5:1). Lime goes #C9E84A → #5E7A12.
The coach card stays dark. One inverted block per screen gives the nudge the weight it needs and keeps the serif voice feeling like a different register.
Recommendation: ship dark as the default, light as a real setting. This is not a filter over the dark theme — it's a second ramp, and the accents differ.

What changed and why
Warm neutrals, not cold charcoal. Every grey gains a little red. Off-white ink instead of pure white. This is where "inviting" comes from.
Two accents with jobs. Amber = act (start, log, confirm). Lime = progress (rings, PRs, on-track). The old app used one lime for all nine jobs.
Two voices. A serif that speaks and a grotesque that measures. The old app had one dense sans at 10–14px.
Space and scale. One thing is allowed to be big on every screen. 24px gutters, 20–28px between blocks, cards without borders — separated by surface, not hairlines.
