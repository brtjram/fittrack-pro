Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

FitTrack Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and encrypted password when you create an account.
  • Profile Data: Age, gender, height, weight, fitness goals, and training preferences you provide in Settings.
  • Fitness Data: Workout sessions, exercise sets, personal records, food log entries, and weight entries you create within the app.
  • Apple Health Data: With your explicit permission, we read steps, active calories, resting heart rate, and body weight from Apple Health. This data is only used to populate your analytics dashboard.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Device push tokens are stored to deliver workout and nutrition reminders you configure.

How We Use Your Information

  • Generate personalized workout plans based on your profile and training history.
  • Calculate macro targets and adaptive nutrition recommendations.
  • Display analytics, trends, and smart insights on your dashboard.
  • Send push notifications for workout reminders, meal logging, and weekly weigh-ins.
  • Sync data across your devices when signed in.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a secure cloud database (Turso) and is encrypted in transit via HTTPS. Authentication tokens are stored in iOS Keychain via Expo SecureStore. We do not sell, share, or provide your data to third parties.

Apple Health

HealthKit data is only accessed when you explicitly enable Apple Health integration in Settings. We read (never write) the following data types: step count, active energy burned, body mass, and heart rate. This data is transmitted securely to your account's server-side storage and is never shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

  • USDA FoodData Central: Food search queries are sent to the USDA API via our server. No personal information is included in these requests.
  • Expo Push Notification Service: Push tokens are registered with Expo's service to deliver notifications to your device.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at the support email below.

Your Rights

You can export, modify, or request deletion of your data at any time. Contact us at support@fittrackpro.app for any privacy-related requests.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@fittrackpro.app.
